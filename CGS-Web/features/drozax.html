Bio

Drozax began writing music in 2019 and built a sound inspired by the atmosphere and emotional pull of classic chillsynth. Their work draws from artists like HOME, Voyage, Hotel Pools, and Eagle Eye Tiger, and carries the same kind of reflective late-night energy that defines the genre.

In May 2024, Drozax released their first album, Reflections. Later that year, on December 2, 2024, the track Sequence from their second album was featured by Electronic Gems, placing their music alongside artists that helped shape their original inspiration.
