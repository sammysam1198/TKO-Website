Chroma Glow

Chillsynth • Synthwave • Ambient Electronic

Chroma Glow is a chillsynth and electronic music artist based in Harvard, Massachusetts. Their work blends synthwave textures, ambient sound design, and emotional melodic composition, creating immersive sonic environments inspired by nostalgia, memory, and atmosphere.
