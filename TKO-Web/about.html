About the Game

WE ARE T.K.O!

T.K.O! The Knockouts is a two-button narrative rhythm game inspired by both rhythm games and fighting games.

The Knockouts are an indie band making noise rock despite all the signs saying they shouldn’t. Or, they were, until the keyboardist / vocalist Hazel left the band to pursue a more stable career in fighting.

With Hazel having risen to the top of the fighting world, the only idea Kit has to get her back in the band is to rise through the ranks herself.

Story Mode

Do It Loud

The story mode is split into two segments - fighting people in tournaments to the beat of T.K.O!’s unreleased album tracks, and spending time with the people you fight outside of the ring and getting to know them better.

The story is an emotionally charged, character / dialogue driven narrative about passion. The hand-drawn cutscenes and animations are inspired by comics and anime, while still aiming to be uniquely personal to us and the story.

The fights are straightforward. Press F to punch, press D to dodge. The bars on the right side of the screen are for punching, bars on the left for dodging.

Sounds simple, right? Well, it is, on the easier difficulties. But this music is FAST, and if you have what it takes to play on the hardest difficulties, you’ll have to be fast too.

In the time leading up to these tournament events, you can spend your days in the city talking to and learning more about the people you’ve fought. You can learn about their passions and struggles, help them through their problems, and unlock new songs for the arcade mode by finishing their stories.

Some of these character events will even have unique rhythm minigames for you to play! Or you could just sleep 24 hours a day until the next bracket starts, I guess.

As you progress through the game and win the tournaments, you may discover that not everyone wants you to go through with this and get Hazel back…

Arcade Mode

If you just want to get right into the rhythm game action, Arcade Mode is the place for you. Pick any song you’ve unlocked and fight any of the fighters you’ve beaten in the story.

You can also use the unlock-all feature to have everything available to you without playing in the story if you hate my writing that bad.

The fighting gameplay is the same as the story, though some tracks’ charts will be adjusted to be more streamlined in this mode, as there won’t be cutscenes or story beats present in these versions.

Characters

The Knockouts

Who exactly are The Knockouts? Well, let’s introduce them.

Kit

Kit is the guitarist for T.K.O!, as well as the character you play as. She’s blunt and has a hard time putting up with some of the more.. eccentric people she meets, but she’s always determined to understand people and be there for them. She’s also an incredible fighter, having trained in a fighting club in high school, although her form has never been very professional.

Hazel

Hazel was the keyboardist / vocalist for T.K.O! before she left the band to pursue fighting. She has always excelled in fighting, having been in the same club as Kit and being the only one who could beat her. They formed a band after high school, but despite being so close, Hazel suddenly left to find a career in fighting. She said it was because they weren’t making a livable amount of money, but it might not be that simple…

The Creators

T.K.O! is being made by a group of people who are incredibly passionate about its music, story, gameplay, art…. Everything. These are the people who are making these things happen!

Strummer “Szoob” Icenogle (Artist / Writer / Concepts / Gameplay Design)

Hey it’s me szoob. I’m the one writing the text on this website. I’m also doing the writing and art for T.K.O!. I came up with the initial concept after Bela said she wanted to make a game with my help, and things have kind of taken off from there. but yeah, I came up with the story, I’m writing the dialogue, I’m the concept / character designer, art director and a part of the animation process… Basically all the stuff you see is me in some way. I just really love making art and stories, but this is the first time I’ll ever be putting one out into the world, and I’m doing my best to make it the best it can be (within reason). god im tired.

Also I don’t like using capital letters, so writing this website has been a nightmare.

Bela Memory (Composer / Lyricist / Programmer / Gameplay Design)

Bela is the composer and programmer for T.K.O!. She’s started a few solo game projects that will be made EventuallyTM, but this is the one she’s working on until it’s done. She’s so on top of her work that she spends a lot of her time waiting to be able to do something (sorry). Pulling back the curtain a bit, she also records the guitar for the game’s music. We swear it’s actually Kit though Kit is real.
