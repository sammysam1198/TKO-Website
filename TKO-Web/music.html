Artist Profile

Chroma Glow

Synthwave / Chillsynth

Sammi, the multi-instrumentalist and Berklee student behind Chroma Glow, started the project as an experiment. Chroma Glow was initially an EDM idea, but upon the creation of singles such as Return, Aestas Stella and later Airstream '91 Sammi quickly gained a small following that expanded with singles like Quattro, Buoyancy and the later 10 song album F A L L.
