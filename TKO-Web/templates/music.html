Featured project

Chroma Glow

Chroma Glow started as an experiment in a one-bedroom apartment and has grown into a highly regarded project by Sammi Fishbein, now working from a home studio near Worcester, Massachusetts. Drawing inspiration from unexpected genres, Sammi blends melodic basslines with breezy synth textures. With influences ranging from metal to jazz, Chroma Glow bridges musical worlds and creates a sound that feels both nostalgic and forward-thinking.
